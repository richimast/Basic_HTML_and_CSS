History of Zebra

Zebras are African equids that diverged from other horse ancestors about 2 to 4 million years ago, originating from a North American ancestor (Hyracotherium) before migrating.

They evolved distinct black-and-white stripes for thermoregulation, predator confusion, and protection against biting flies.

There are three distinct species—Plains, Mountain, and Grévy's—found in Eastern and Southern Africa.
